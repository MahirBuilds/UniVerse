How to Play Pinball Rebounds

Overview: Pinball Rebounds is a high-speed neuro-spatial puzzle combining visual memory, spatial reasoning, and optical reflection physics.

1. The 6-Second Memorization Phase

When a round begins, a 4×4 grid of diagonal mirrors is revealed alongside the laser entrance point. You have 6 seconds to commit the mirror matrix to memory before all mirrors disappear!

2. Mirror Physics & Deflection Rules

/ Forward Slash (Cyan)
• Entering Left → Deflects UP
• Entering Right → Deflects DOWN
• Entering Top → Deflects LEFT
• Entering Bottom → Deflects RIGHT
\ Backslash (Blue)
• Entering Left → Deflects DOWN
• Entering Right → Deflects UP
• Entering Top → Deflects RIGHT
• Entering Bottom → Deflects LEFT

3. Boundary Coordinate Identifiers

The 4×4 perimeter features 16 nodes:
• T0 – T3: Top perimeter (columns 0 to 3)
• B0 – B3: Bottom perimeter (columns 0 to 3)
• L0 – L3: Left perimeter (rows 0 to 3)
• R0 – R3: Right perimeter (rows 0 to 3)

Select your predicted exit node either by clicking directly on the perimeter markers or tapping the choice pills below, then hit Submit Prediction to unleash the laser!
